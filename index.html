1968 — 2024

A history in progress

Hover or focus on a card to reveal the milestone.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting a revolution in computing.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 establishes the x86 architecture that drives countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

1985

386 Processor

The 386 processor introduces 32-bit architecture and ushers in a new era of performance and multitasking.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches its highest operational greenhouse gas emissions, prompting major investment in abatement and efficiency.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling strategy and 2030 goals.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions across global operations by 2040.

2023

Renewable Electricity

The company reaches 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, lowering carbon emissions across operations.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit to collaborate on next-generation sustainable manufacturing.
